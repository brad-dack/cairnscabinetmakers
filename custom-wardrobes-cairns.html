Custom and Built-In Wardrobes in Cairns

The choices behind a built-in wardrobe, and what to tell a Cairns cabinet maker so the quote matches the room.

Get a wardrobe quoteCall (07) 4028 4041

    A custom wardrobe is built for one wall in one room, which is why two quotes for "a built-in robe" can describe quite different pieces of work. Before asking for prices, it helps to settle four things: the type of wardrobe, the doors, the layout inside, and how the space will be measured.

    Built-in, walk-in or a new fit-out

    A built-in wardrobe fills a recess or runs along a wall from floor to ceiling, with doors across the front. A walk-in wardrobe is a small room lined with hanging space, shelves and drawers, usually with no doors on the inside fittings at all. A fit-out keeps the existing wardrobe shell and doors and replaces only what is inside.

    If the bedroom already has a robe recess, say in your enquiry whether you want it rebuilt, refitted inside, or left alone with the new wardrobe on another wall.

    Hinged or sliding doors

    Hinged doors open the whole width of the wardrobe at once, but need clear floor in front of them to swing. Sliding doors take no floor space when open, run on a top or bottom track, and only ever show part of the inside at one time. Sliding panels can be timber-look, painted or mirrored.

    If the room has a bed, a door or a window close to the wardrobe, measure how much space there is in front of it before deciding. That one measurement often settles the question.

    Planning the inside

    The inside layout is where a custom wardrobe earns its price. Start from what you own rather than from a picture: how much hangs long, how much hangs short, how much folds, and how many pairs of shoes there are. Then ask for a drawing that shows where each of those goes.

    • Long hanging for dresses and coats, and double hanging rails for shirts and trousers
    • Adjustable shelves, and how far apart they can be set
    • Drawers, and whether they are inside the doors or on show
    • Shoe racks, a tie or belt rail, and space for luggage up high
    • Lighting inside, which brings in an electrician as well as the cabinet maker

    Measuring the space

    Walls and ceilings are rarely square, and a wardrobe built to the inch has to allow for that. A cabinet maker will measure the space themselves before giving a firm price, and will want to know about skirting boards, cornices, power points, light switches and air-conditioning vents on the wall the wardrobe will cover.

    What those months mean for a wardrobe is a question for the cabinet maker, not a rule this page can give you. Questions worth asking:

    • Will the wardrobe sit against an outside wall, and does that change how you build it?
    • Is there any gap or airflow behind the back panel or through the doors?
    • What are the carcass and shelves made of, and what finish goes on the inside faces?
    • What are the sliding tracks or hinges made of?

    Before you agree to a price

    Queensland's building regulator, the QBCC, says building work valued over $3,300 needs a QBCC licence, and it has a Cabinet making licence class for installing and repairing fitted cabinets on site. Ask for the licence number before you agree to a job over that value.

    For domestic work over $3,300 the QBCC also says a written, dated and signed contract is essential. The usual deposit limits have an exception of up to 20% where more than half the price is made-to-measure work done away from your home, and the example the QBCC gives is cabinetry. The bands and the rest of the detail are on the kitchen renovations in Cairns page.

    What to include when you enquire

    • Which room, and the width and height of the wall or recess, roughly
    • Built-in, walk-in, or a new fit-out of an existing robe
    • Hinged or sliding doors, mirrored or not, if you know
    • What will go in it: long hanging, folded clothes, shoes, linen
    • Whether an old wardrobe has to come out first
    • The suburb, and when you would like it done

    Rough numbers are fine. The cabinet maker measures properly before the quote is final.

    Common questions

    How much does a built-in wardrobe cost in Cairns?

    This page does not give a figure, because there is no Cairns source for one. The price turns on the width, the doors, how much is inside and what it is made of, and a cabinet maker can price it once they have measured.

    Can you just replace the doors on my existing wardrobe?

    Often, if the shell is sound and the opening is a standard shape. Say so in your enquiry, with a photo if you can, and the cabinet maker can tell you whether doors alone will do.

    Is a sliding door or a hinged door better?

    Neither in every room. Sliding doors suit tight spaces in front of the wardrobe; hinged doors show everything at once. The space in front of the wardrobe usually decides it.

    Do I need to empty the room for the measure?

    No. The cabinet maker needs to reach the wall, the floor and the ceiling where the wardrobe will go, so clearing that strip is enough.
